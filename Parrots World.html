Quick Parrot Facts

  • Longevity

    Some species live up to 80 years.


  • Intelligence

    Parrots have the emotional intelligence of a 4-year-old child.


  • Unique Feet

    They have zygodactyl feet with 2 toes facing front and 2 facing back.

Welcome to The World of Parrots

Parrots are among the most intelligent and colorful birds on Earth. Belonging to the order Psittaciformes, they thrive mostly in tropical and subtropical regions worldwide.

Why Parrots Are Special

Known for their vibrant plumage, powerful curved beaks, and remarkable ability to mimic human speech, parrots form complex social bonds and require deep mental stimulation.
